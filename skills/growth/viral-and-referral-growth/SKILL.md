# Viral & Referral Growth

## Purpose

Help an AI mentor evaluate whether a product or service can grow through sharing, recommendations, invitations, or customer referrals—and design a useful, trustworthy referral mechanism when appropriate.

## Distinguish the mechanisms

- **Organic word of mouth:** Customers recommend an offer because of their experience.
- **Referral program:** The business deliberately makes it easier to recommend and may provide a disclosed incentive.
- **Viral loop:** Product use or a user action naturally exposes the product to potential new users, who may then become users themselves.
- **Content sharing:** Useful, entertaining, or identity-relevant content is shared beyond the original audience.

These mechanisms overlap but are not interchangeable. A referral reward does not automatically create a viral product.

## Design process

1. Identify the customer value that makes sharing worthwhile.
2. Identify the natural moment when a satisfied customer might recommend or invite someone.
3. Reduce friction in the sharing or invitation process.
4. Make the invitation understandable and honest.
5. If incentives exist, disclose terms and prevent abuse.
6. Measure invitations, successful referrals, activation, paid conversion, and referred-customer quality.
7. Compare referral economics with other acquisition channels.
8. Improve the customer experience that creates genuine willingness to recommend.

## Useful metrics

- Share or invitation rate.
- Referral conversion rate.
- Referred-user activation rate.
- Referred-user retention and repeat purchase.
- Incentive cost per acquired customer.
- Fraud, abuse, complaints, and refund rates.

A viral coefficient can be estimated when reliable data exists, but do not infer sustainable virality from a temporary spike or a high sharing count.

## AI decision rules

- Do not assume every business can become viral.
- Do not encourage spam invitations, contact scraping, unsolicited bulk messaging, or deceptive sharing prompts.
- Do not hide referral incentives or material conditions.
- Do not reward low-quality sign-ups without checking activation, retention, and abuse.
- Improve real customer value before relying on referral mechanics.
- Consider privacy, consent, and platform rules.

## Relationship to other skills

Consumer Psychology & Persuasion supports honest word-of-mouth messaging. Growth Experimentation tests referral mechanics. Growth Metrics & Optimization measures referral quality. Community Building is one of the traction channels that can support sustained advocacy.
