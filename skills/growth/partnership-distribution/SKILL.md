# Partnership & Distribution Strategy

## Purpose

Help an AI mentor identify and evaluate partnerships that can create qualified customer access, complementary value, or more efficient distribution.

## Relevant traction channels

This skill connects several channels from *Traction*:
- Business Development.
- Affiliate Programs.
- Existing Platforms.
- Targeting Blogs.
- Trade Shows and Offline Events where relationships can be established.

Partnerships are not automatically valuable. A partner should provide a credible route to a relevant audience or capability, and the arrangement should create clear value for both parties.

## Partnership evaluation

Assess:
- Audience overlap and relevance.
- Partner credibility and customer trust.
- Complementarity of products or services.
- Incentives and value exchange for each party.
- Expected reach and qualified outcomes.
- Integration, support, and operational effort.
- Commercial terms, attribution, and payment timing.
- Brand, legal, privacy, and platform risks.
- Dependency and exit options.

## Partnership workflow

1. Define the target audience and the gap in current distribution.
2. Identify organizations, creators, platforms, affiliates, or communities with a relevant audience.
3. Research fit and verify claims about reach and engagement.
4. Prepare a concise mutual-value proposal.
5. Agree on scope, responsibilities, disclosures, attribution, data handling, and commercial terms.
6. Start with a small pilot.
7. Measure qualified leads, conversions, retained customers, contribution, and partner effort.
8. Continue, revise, or end the relationship based on results.

## AI decision rules

- Do not invent partner relationships, endorsements, audience sizes, or expected results.
- Disclose paid, affiliate, or incentivized recommendations clearly.
- Do not share customer data without a valid basis and appropriate consent.
- Do not commit the user to contracts, payments, or public announcements without explicit approval.
- Avoid partnerships where the audience is irrelevant or the economics are unsustainable.
- Keep ownership of customer relationships and exit arrangements clear.
