# YouTube Channel Audit & Optimization

## Purpose
Review a channel as a connected system—positioning, audience promise, content consistency, packaging, publishing capacity, and available performance evidence—and identify the single highest-priority improvement.

## Inputs
Channel URL or channel data supplied by the user, recent video list, titles and thumbnails, analytics where available, target audience, goals, and creator capacity.

## Audit sequence
1. Confirm channel identity, niche, intended audience, and desired outcome.
2. Review a recent sample of videos as a set, not only one upload.
3. Evaluate topic consistency and whether each video serves a recognizable audience promise.
4. Review titles and thumbnails at realistic mobile/feed size.
5. Inspect openings and scripts where transcripts are available.
6. Review upload consistency against actual creator capacity.
7. Examine retention, CTR, traffic source, returning viewers, and conversion only when data is supplied.
8. Identify what is already working and should be preserved.
9. Select one bottleneck and one practical fix for the next review cycle.

## Output
- Audit scope, date, and evidence limitations.
- One primary diagnosis and recommended fix.
- Up to three specific strengths worth preserving.
- What not to change yet and why.
- A short action plan and measurement criteria.

## Decision rules
- Do not pretend to inspect private analytics from a public channel URL.
- Do not invent channel metrics or claim a causal diagnosis from surface observations.
- Do not give a long generic list of problems; prioritize one constraint.
- Separate public observations, user-provided analytics, and hypotheses.
- Do not recommend increasing publishing volume when capacity or quality is the constraint.

## Ownership boundary
This skill owns the integrated audit and prioritization. It delegates packaging, retention, script, SEO, and competitor methods to their specialist owners and synthesizes their bounded findings. It does not repeat their full workflows. Follow [Skill Boundary & Routing Governance](../../../orchestrator/skill-boundary-governance/SKILL.md).
