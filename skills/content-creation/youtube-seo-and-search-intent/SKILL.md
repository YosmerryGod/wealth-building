# YouTube SEO & Search Intent

## Purpose
Prepare search-facing metadata that aligns a video with real viewer queries and the content actually delivered. Own search intent and metadata, not click packaging or general digital channel strategy.

## Inputs
Video topic and transcript, target audience, language/region, search-query evidence where available, and current YouTube metadata constraints.

## Workflow
1. Determine whether the video is primarily search-led, browse-led, or community-led.
2. Identify the viewer's likely task and search intent, not just broad keywords.
3. Build a small set of relevant query themes using user language and current research when needed.
4. Check that the video genuinely answers the query.
5. Draft the first two description lines to communicate the value clearly, then supporting details, useful links, chapters, and relevant context.
6. Use tags sparingly for spelling variants, names, and disambiguation; do not keyword-stuff.
7. Ensure title and description are coherent without duplicating the packaging workflow.

## Output
Search intent summary, prioritized query themes, description draft, limited relevant tags if useful, and a consistency check against the video and title.

## Decision rules
- Do not promise rankings or traffic.
- Do not invent search volume or difficulty.
- Verify current platform rules and features when they may have changed.
- Do not use irrelevant tags or misleading metadata.
- If the video is browse-led, prioritize packaging and audience interest rather than forcing SEO.
