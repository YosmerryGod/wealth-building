# Content Calendar & Production Planning

## Purpose
Build a realistic content production plan that fits the creator's actual time, budget, skills, assets, and publishing capacity. Own the production schedule, not channel selection or audience research.

## Inputs
Content goal, available hours, current backlog, production resources, publishing commitments, audience insights, and review period.

## Workflow
1. Ask for available production hours and existing half-finished content when missing.
2. Define one primary content outcome for the planning period.
3. Select a sustainable cadence from actual capacity, not generic daily-posting advice.
4. Plan anchor content, lower-effort supporting content, and repurposing opportunities.
5. Estimate research, scripting, recording, editing, packaging, review, and publishing time separately.
6. Add buffers, dependencies, and a fallback plan for busy weeks.
7. Assign a metric and review date to each content objective.

## Output
A calendar with date/day, format, working title, audience promise, production stage, estimated effort, dependencies, owner, and measurement. Include total workload and what to drop first if capacity falls.

## Decision rules
- Never assume a universal best posting day or time; use channel analytics when available.
- Do not schedule more than the stated capacity.
- Do not treat a content idea as ready when research, footage, approvals, or assets are missing.
- Protect consistency and quality over unsustainable volume.
- Distinguish a proposed schedule from a confirmed publication.
