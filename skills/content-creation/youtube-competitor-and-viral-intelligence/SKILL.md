# YouTube Competitor & Viral Intelligence

## Purpose
Research public videos in a defined niche and identify repeatable content patterns by comparing each video's performance with its own channel baseline where sufficient data exists. Own competitor/video research, not the user's channel audit or content production.

## Inputs
Niche, target audience, geography/language, competitor/channel list or search scope, research date, and public video metrics available.

## Workflow
1. Define the niche and comparison question precisely.
2. Collect public video listings and record source, observation date, title, URL, views, publish date, duration, and channel.
3. Where possible, collect at least four comparable videos per channel before calculating a channel median.
4. Compare a video's views with its own channel's median and report the multiple; do not rank only by raw views.
5. Classify observable title, topic, format, opening, and packaging patterns separately from explanations of performance.
6. Identify recurring patterns across multiple examples and distinguish observations from hypotheses.
7. Recommend only ideas that fit the user's audience, resources, voice, and content promise.

## Output
Research scope and date, source-linked examples, normalized performance comparison where possible, observed patterns, hypotheses, and a small number of feasible content opportunities.

## Decision rules
- Use public information only; never request or use account credentials.
- Respect platform terms and access limits; do not bypass access controls.
- Do not invent metrics or treat title-pattern classification as causal proof.
- State sample size, missing data, and comparability limitations.
- Do not promise virality or copy another creator's distinctive work.
